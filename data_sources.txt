# ValidDs Data Source Mapping (Discovery 2.0)

Every field in the ValidDs product schema is sourced from the most reliable market intelligence provider.

## 1. Product Discovery & Content
- title: AI Extraction (Sanitized from TikTok/SerpApi)
- description: AI Extraction (High-converting product description)
- categoryL1 / categoryL2 / categoryL3: AI Extraction (3-Level Taxonomy)
- hashtags: TikTok (Original Viral Post)
- primaryImageUrl: SerpApi (178x133 Immersive Search)
- imageUrls: SerpApi (Full High-Res Gallery - Primary Source)
- videoUrl: TikTok (Direct media .mp4 link)

## 2. Market Intelligence (SerpApi / AI)
- unitsSold: AI Grounded Search (Amazon/Global estimates)
- salesSource: AI Grounded Search (Store name, proves volume, direct link)
- ratingSources: SerpApi (Multi-platform ratings with source URLs) OR estimated from TikTok engagement
- relatedProducts: SerpApi (Google "Immersive Products" carousel)

## 3. Social Proof & Engagement (TikTok / EnsembleData)
- totalViews / totalLikes / totalComments / shares: TikTok (Real-time engagement)
- engagementRate: EnsembledData/Calculated (Normalized engagement)
- primaryCreator: TikTok (Original Viral Video Author & Link)
- topComments: EnsembleData (Real representative comments & AI sentiment)
- Creatives (Sub-Collection): EnsembleData (100 TikTok videos per product, each mapped to a creator)

## 4. AI Logic & Confidence
- Discovery Sections: Discovery Service (Internal Algorithm: trending, top-ads, etc.)
- extractionConfidence / Reason: AI Extraction (DeepSeek/Gemini dynamic assessment)
- buyingSentiment / Reason: AI Extraction (Comment analysis for purchase intent)
- trendScore / Direction / Reason: AI Extraction (Growth trajectory analysis)

## 5. Supplier Data (TeemDrop)
- price: TeemDrop (Direct supplier price)
- currency: TeemDrop
- suppliers: TeemDrop (Verified dropshipping partners)
